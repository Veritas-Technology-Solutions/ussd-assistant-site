Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

USSDAssistant (the “App”) is built and distributed by Veritas Technology Solutions. This page explains how we handle your information when you use the App.

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.
The App works completely offline. It does not request the internet permission, and it cannot connect to the internet or send data anywhere.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, location).
  • No device identifiers (beyond the temporary product‑key validation).
  • No usage analytics, crash logs, or advertising identifiers.
  • No financial data – the App never sees your M‑Pesa PIN or any payment details.
  • No voice recordings – the optional voice feature processes speech locally on‑device and never stores or transmits audio.

2. Offline Operation

The App does not have the INTERNET permission. This means it is technically incapable of connecting to the internet. All data (USSD flows, activation status, etc.) is stored only on your device in private storage that no other app can access.

3. Accessibility Service

USSDAssistant requires the Accessibility Service for its core function: automating USSD menu navigation. Android provides no other way to read USSD pop‑up text or simulate menu taps.

The Accessibility Service is used only when you explicitly tap “Run” on a flow. It reads only the text of USSD dialog windows and clicks only the buttons you have configured. It does not observe any other app, keystrokes, or sensitive screens like your PIN entry. The service automatically stops before the PIN/password screen.

4. Product‑Key Activation

The App requires a one‑time product‑key to activate. During activation, a unique device identifier (Android ID) is used to generate and validate the key. This identifier never leaves your device (validation happens entirely on‑device). You share it with us voluntarily via WhatsApp to receive your key. We do not store or associate this identifier with any other information.

5. No Background Processes

The App has no background services, no automatic startup, and no hidden processes. It only runs when you open it. You can revoke the Accessibility permission at any time from Android Settings, and the App will stop functioning.

6. Third‑Party Services

The App does not integrate any third‑party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The optional voice feature uses your device’s built‑in speech recognizer (Google Speech Services), which processes speech locally when an offline language pack is installed. No voice data is sent to Google’s servers when offline.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, the new version will be posted here. We will not reduce your privacy rights without your explicit consent.
